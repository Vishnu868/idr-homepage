About IDR

Bridging academic insight with industry practice

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute specialising in digital, cyber and technology risk roles. We prepare practitioners to operate in regulated, high-consequence environments — from financial services to critical national infrastructure.

Through a unique partnership model with leading UK universities, IDR combines rigorous academic research with real-world project experience. Our graduates don't just understand risk frameworks — they've applied them under live conditions before day one on the job.

Our Model

Three pillars. One mission.

IDR operates at the intersection of training, innovation and advisory — ensuring organisations have both the talent and the frameworks to manage digital risk at scale.

Academy

Intensive bootcamps and structured programmes for students, graduates and professionals. Our training covers cyber risk fundamentals, AI governance, and regulatory compliance — accredited through UK university partners.

View programmes →

Innovation & Incubation

Developing intellectual property, future risk models and AI governance frameworks. Our research lab works with industry partners to tackle emerging threats — from adversarial AI to quantum-readiness in critical infrastructure.

Explore research →

Advisory Services

Helping organisations implement and mature their risk frameworks. Our advisors bring deep experience in NIST CSF, ISO 27001, NIS2 and sector-specific regulation across financial services and critical infrastructure.
