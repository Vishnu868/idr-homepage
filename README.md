# Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) – Homepage Design

This project is a responsive homepage design for the Institute of Digital Risk (IDR), an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on digital, cyber and AI risk.

## Logo Design

The IDR logo uses a geometric cube to represent structure, resilience and layered digital risk systems. The cube symbol reflects stability and protection in complex technological environments. The orange colour conveys innovation and energy, while black and white provide a clean, professional technology aesthetic. The minimalist typography ensures clarity and legibility across both desktop and mobile environments.

## Homepage Features

• Hero section with mission statement and CTA
• About IDR section explaining academic and industry collaboration
• Three service pillars: Academy, Innovation & Incubation, Advisory Services
• Pipeline model (Train → Hire → Innovate → Deploy)
• Community section describing who IDR serves
• Contact / Register Interest form

## Technical Implementation

• Semantic HTML5 structure
• Responsive layout using CSS Flexbox/Grid
• Sticky navigation with smooth scrolling
• Accessible colour palette (orange, white, black)
• Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS (no frameworks)
